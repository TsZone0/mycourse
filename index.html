Why Choose Us

Everything a serious student needs

Built by a student, for students. No distractions. Just pure focused learning.

YouTube Course Organizer

Add any YouTube lecture link and watch inside a clean, distraction-free player. No recommended videos. No algorithm traps.

Study Heatmap

GitHub-style heatmap shows your daily consistency over 12 months. Watch your green streak grow and stay motivated.

📝

Per-Lecture Notes

Write and save notes for every lecture. Stored securely in the cloud — access from anywhere, any device.

Pomodoro Timer

Built-in 25/5 Pomodoro timer keeps you in deep focus mode. Visual ring counts down and auto-switches between work and break sessions.

Flashcard Maker

Create flip-to-reveal Q&A flashcards for any lecture. Perfect for quick revision before exams.

Weekly Study Chart

Daily Goal Tracker

Set your daily study tasks and tick them off. Clarity on what to do next keeps you in flow.

Sticky Notes Dashboard

Pin quick thoughts, reminders, and ideas as colorful sticky notes right on your dashboard.

Cloud Sync

All data saved on the cloud. Login from phone, laptop, or tablet — everything always synced.
